The Concept

What is Vinyl Night?

Learn more →

Vinyl Night is a monthly ritual built around the album — one of the greatest forms of music media ever made. At each gathering, members convene with records and drinks to share, reveal three independently selected albums, determine the listening order together, and proceed to appreciate three complete record albums. After eight years and not a single missed month, we thought we might enshrine the Vinyl Night format for others to enjoy.
