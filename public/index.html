The Concept

What is Vinyl Night?

Learn more →

Vinyl Night is a monthly ritual built around the album — one of the greatest forms of music media ever made. Each person brings a record and a few drinks to share. The group listens to three albums in full, determines the order together, and discusses what they heard. Pre-music when people arrive. Post-music at the end. What happens in between is the official record. After eight years and not a single missed month, we can tell you: three is the magic number.
